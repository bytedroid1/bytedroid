What our own apps have in common

Hasp Vault, TrustScan, PDF Send and InvoiceBoss.

  • No account.

    None of them asks you to create an account or sign up with us.

  • Your things stay with you.

    Your documents, scans, invoices and files are never uploaded to us. They stay on your phone, or in your own iCloud or Google Drive if you turn on sync.

  • Free where it counts.

    Each one does its main job for free. Pro adds extras, and it is always optional.

  • No internet needed.

    The parts that matter work offline, on the phone itself. PDF Send needs only your own Wi-Fi or hotspot.
