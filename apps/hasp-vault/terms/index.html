By installing or using Hasp Vault (“the app”) you agree to these terms. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the app.

1. What the app does

Hasp Vault stores documents, passwords, bank cards and notes locally on your device. It is a storage and organisation tool. It is not a password manager service, a backup service, or a cloud product, and it does not transmit your content to us.

If you turn on Sync (Hasp Pro, version 1.3.0 and later), the app keeps an encrypted copy of your vault in your own iCloud or Google Drive. That storage is provided by Apple or Google under their own terms, in your own account; we never receive or hold that copy.

2. Your data is your responsibility

Please read this section carefully — it is the most important one here.

  • Everything you store lives on your device — and, if you turn on Sync, as an encrypted copy in your own iCloud or Google Drive. We hold no copy and cannot recover anything for you.
  • Without Sync, uninstalling the app permanently destroys your vault. So does erasing the app’s data, resetting the phone, or losing it.
  • With Sync on, the copy in your cloud is only as safe as your Apple or Google account, because the key that opens it is kept there too. Keeping that account secure is your responsibility.
  • If you forget your PIN, your vault cannot be opened. There is no reset code, no recovery question and no back door. This is deliberate — a way in for you would be a way in for someone else.
  • If you forget the passphrase on an exported backup, that backup cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us.
  • Keeping backups is your responsibility. We strongly recommend exporting one and storing it somewhere safe.

3. Free plan and Hasp Pro

The app is free to use, with no limit on how many documents, passwords, cards or notes you store. Encrypted backup and restore are included free, deliberately, so that protecting yourself from data loss never costs money.

Hasp Pro is an optional upgrade that unlocks additional features — Sync between your devices (version 1.3.0 and later), folders, expiry reminders, the password health check, phone-to-phone transfer, break-in alerts, the intruder photo and extra themes. It is sold two ways, and both unlock exactly the same features:

  • Lifetime — a single payment, owned permanently. It never renews and never expires.
  • Yearly — a subscription that renews automatically each year until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time from your App Store or Google Play account; cancelling stops future renewals and Pro stays active until the period you have already paid for ends.

The yearly plan may be offered with a free trial — three days on Google Play at the time of writing. The trial costs nothing, and if you cancel before it ends you are not charged. If you do not cancel, the yearly subscription begins automatically at the price shown in the app when you signed up, and renews each year until cancelled. The trial is offered only to accounts that have not subscribed to Hasp Pro before, so the store decides whether it applies to you; the app always shows the exact terms and the renewing price before you confirm.

Purchases and cancellations are handled by the App Store or Google Play; refunds are subject to their policies, not ours. Because the app has no accounts and no servers, your purchase is restored by the “Restore purchases” button, which asks the store what your account owns.

The app contains no advertising. Should advertising ever be introduced, it would be optional, it would never appear alongside your documents, cards or passwords, and these terms and the privacy policy would be updated before that version was released.
