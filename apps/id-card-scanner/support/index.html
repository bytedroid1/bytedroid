The camera does not find my card

Lay the card flat on a plain surface that is darker than the card, such as a table, and hold the phone so the card fills most of the white frame. Bright light from one side can hide the card’s edges, so try turning a little away from a lamp or window.

If it still does not find it, tap the big button to take the photo yourself. If the app cannot see the card’s edges in that photo, it keeps what is inside the white frame. You can turn off Auto scan in Settings if you would rather always take the photo yourself.

Where are my documents kept?

On your phone, inside the app. They are not added to your photos, they are kept out of iCloud and Google backups, and they are never sent to us. See the privacy policy for the few things that do leave the phone.

Moving to a new phone

Your documents do not come with you in your phone’s backup. The app keeps them out of iCloud and Google backups, so that they are never uploaded. Scan them again on the new phone. If you have PRO, open the PRO screen on the new phone and tap Restore.

Before you give away or sell the old phone, delete the app on it, or erase the phone. That removes the documents.

Can I use a copy as ID?

No. A copy you share is stamped COPY and NOT VALID AS ID, and it is meant for the times a bank, a hotel, a landlord or an employer asks you for a copy of your document. Whether a copy is accepted is up to whoever asks for it.

Hiding a detail on a copy

On Share a copy, tap or drag across any detail on the card to black it out. Tap a black bar to bring the detail back. Your saved document is not changed; only the copy is.

Expiry reminders

With PRO, turn on Expiry reminders in Settings. The app then reminds you 60 days and 14 days before a document expires, and on the day, at 9 in the morning, for every document with an expiry date. The phone asks once whether the app may show notifications; if you said no, turn them on for ID Card Scanner in your phone’s own Settings.

The app lock

With PRO, turn on App lock in Settings. The app then asks for Face ID, a fingerprint or your phone’s code each time it opens, and again when you come back to it after 30 seconds or more. While it is in the background, its picture in the app switcher shows no document.

The lock uses your phone’s own screen lock, so if Face ID or your fingerprint isn’t recognised, your phone’s code always opens it. To turn the lock on, the phone needs a screen lock set.

I paid for PRO but it is not showing

Open the PRO screen and tap Restore, signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. A lifetime purchase never needs buying twice.

If it still does not appear, email us with the store you bought from and the date, and we will help you chase it.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us.

  • iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: Play Store, then your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.

You keep PRO until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If PRO ends, you keep every document you already have, and the free limit of 3 applies again when you add a new one.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from, not by us. Apple’s are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com, and Google’s from your purchase history in the Play Store. Write to us if you get stuck.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a small banner on the Documents screen, and offers a short video ad if you want to make one PDF without PRO. The ads come from Google, and the app asks for non-personalised ads only. PRO has no ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you can change your choice about ads at any time in Settings, under Privacy choices for ads.
