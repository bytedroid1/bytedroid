At a glance

Account
None. No sign-up.
Your documents
Scans and the details you type stay on your phone and are never sent to us. The app keeps them out of iCloud and Google backups. A copy leaves the phone only when you share one yourself.
Advertising
Google AdMob, in the free version only. It shows a small banner, and a short video ad if you choose to watch one to make a PDF. The app asks for non-personalised ads only, after Google's consent form where the law requires one. PRO has no ads.
Crash reports and usage
We use no analytics or crash-reporting tools.
Purchases
Through the App Store or Google Play, checked by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier. We never see card details.

The short version: your documents stay on your phone. We never receive them. There is no account, and no server of ours for them to go to. This page covers the parts that do leave the phone.

Stays on your phone

  • Every scan, front and back, and the details you type: the title, the name, the document number and the expiry date
  • The camera’s live picture. The app looks for the card on the phone itself, and only the sides you keep are saved
  • Photos you import. The app saves its own copy, cut to the document’s shape, and leaves your photo as it was

The location, the phone model and the other details a camera adds to a photo are removed from every saved scan, including photos you import.

The app also keeps your documents out of your phone’s iCloud and Google backups, so they are never uploaded with the rest of your phone. This means they do not come back from a backup on a new phone: scan them again there.

Leaves your phone

  • A copy you share, only when you share it, and only to the app or the person you choose. A copy carries no name, location or account of yours.
  • Advertising in the free version, via Google AdMob: a small banner on the Documents screen, and a short video ad if you choose to watch one to make a PDF. The app asks Google for non-personalised ads only. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may receive your advertising ID, your IP address, your device model and system version, which ads you see or tap, and diagnostic details about the ads. Your documents are never shared with Google. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings, under Privacy choices for ads. PRO has no ads. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Purchases via RevenueCat. The App Store or Google Play takes the payment, and we never see card details. RevenueCat receives the store’s receipt and an anonymous ID for the app on your phone, so that PRO can be restored. It never receives your documents. RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

We use no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we sell no information.

Permissions and why

  • Camera, to scan your documents. Nothing is recorded, and only the sides you keep are saved.
  • Photos, only when you import a photo or save a copy to your photos. The app sees only the photo you pick.

Your choices

Delete a document in the app and it is gone from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Children

ID Card Scanner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
