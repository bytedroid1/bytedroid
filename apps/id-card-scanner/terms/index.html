A copy made with the app is not an official document and is not valid as ID. Every copy you share is stamped to say so. Whether a bank, a hotel, a landlord, an employer or anyone else accepts a copy is up to them, and some places have rules about copying identity documents. Following the rules where you are is your responsibility.

4. Free version and PRO

The free version is not a trial and does not expire. It keeps up to 3 documents and shows ads. Sharing copies as images is free; a PDF needs PRO, or one short video ad for each PDF.

ID Card Scanner PRO keeps as many documents as you like, makes PDFs without ads, removes the ads, reminds you before documents expire, and can lock the app. If PRO ends, you keep every document you already have, and the free limit applies again when you add a new one. Reminders stop, and an app lock you turned on stays on until you turn it off.
