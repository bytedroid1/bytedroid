At a glance

Account
None. No sign-up.
Your cards
Cards, photos, logos, signatures and the spreadsheets you import stay on your phone and are never sent to us. A card leaves the phone only when you save, share or print it yourself.
Photo tools
Face framing and background removal run on the phone. On Android, Google's ML Kit reports basic performance figures to Google; your photo is not sent.
Advertising
Google AdMob, in the free version only. A small banner under the main screens, now and then a full-screen ad after you save, share or print a card, and a short video only if you choose one. On iPhone, non-personalised ads only and no tracking. Your cards are never shared. Pro has no ads.
Crash reports and usage
Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics receive crash reports and anonymous usage counts, such as whether the Pro offer is opened or bought. Never your cards, photos or anything you type, and you can turn them off in Settings.
Purchases
Through the App Store or Google Play, checked by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier. We never see card details.

The short version: your cards stay on your phone. We never receive them. There is no account, and no server of ours for them to go to. This page covers the parts that do leave the phone.

Stays on your phone

  • Every card you make, front and back, and the details you type: names, job titles, ID numbers, dates, blood groups, emergency contacts and any field you add
  • Photos, logos, signatures and background pictures you add. The app saves its own copy, sized for the card, and leaves your original as it was
  • Spreadsheets you import to make cards for many people. The app reads the rows into the batch and does not keep the file
  • Organisations you save, with their names, logos and addresses
  • Face framing and background removal. They run on the phone itself: on iPhone with Apple’s Vision, and on Android with Google’s ML Kit and Android’s own face detector. The photo is not uploaded to do this

Like any app’s data, your cards are included in your phone’s own iCloud or Google backup if you have backups turned on. They are never sent to us.

Leaves your phone

  • Cards you save, share or print, only when you do it, and only to the place you choose: your photos, a printer, or the app or person you share with.
  • Advertising in the free version, via Google AdMob: a small banner under the main screens, now and then a full-screen ad after you save, share or print a card (never while you are making one), and a short video only if you choose to watch one instead of buying Pro for a single action. On Android, where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings, under Privacy choices for ads. On iPhone, the app asks for non-personalised ads only, shows no ads where consent would be required, and never asks to track you. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may receive your advertising ID (on Android), your IP address, your device model and system version, which ads you see or tap, and diagnostic details about the ads. Your cards are never shared with Google. Pro has no ads. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Crash reports and usage counts, via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics: when something goes wrong, a crash report with the device model and system version; and anonymous counts of which screens are opened, which ways of saving and printing are used, and whether the Pro offer is opened or bought. Never a name, a photo, a card or anything you type. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland they stay off unless you agree, and anyone can turn them off in Settings, under Share usage counts and crash reports. Turning them off also resets the random ID they use. Firebase’s privacy information.
  • Google ML Kit, on Android only. When you remove a photo’s background on Android, Google’s ML Kit does the work on the phone. It also reports basic performance and error figures to Google, with its own random install ID. The photo itself is not sent.
  • Purchases via RevenueCat. The App Store or Google Play takes the payment, and we never see card details. RevenueCat receives the store’s receipt and an anonymous ID for the app on your phone, so that Pro can be restored. It never receives your cards. RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

We sell no information.

Permissions and why

  • Camera, only when you choose to take a picture for a card: a portrait, a logo, a signature or a background. Nothing is recorded. On Android, your phone’s own camera app takes the picture and hands it to IDcrest.
  • Photos. Choosing a photo uses your phone’s photo picker, and the app sees only the photos you pick. Tapping Save to Photos asks once to add pictures to your library; the app does not read your library to do this. On Android 10 and older, saving asks for storage access instead.

Cards about other people

When you make cards for other people, such as your staff, students or guests, their details and photos stay on your phone too, and you decide what goes on each card and who receives it. Please add people’s details only with their permission, and keep them as the law where you are requires.

Your choices

Delete a card or a batch in the app and it is removed. The photos it used are cleared from the app’s storage within a few days, which leaves time to undo a mistake. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Usage counts and crash reports can be turned off in Settings at any time. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings, and change your choice about ads in Settings, under Privacy choices for ads, where that choice applies.

Children

IDcrest is made for the people who make cards, such as offices, schools and event organisers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
