The printed card is the wrong size

In your printer’s options, choose Actual size or 100%. “Fit to page” or “Scale to fit” shrinks the card. A standard ID card is 85.6 × 54 mm, the size of a bank card, and IDcrest’s files print at exactly that size. You can choose A4 or US Letter paper in Settings, under Printing.

Printing both sides of a card

Choose Print on paper, then how the back is printed:

  • Front and back side by side works on any printer. Cut out both and laminate them back to back.
  • Two-sided printing puts the backs on the next page, lined up behind their fronts, for printers that print both sides. If the backs come out upside down, turn on Printer flips on the short edge.

Print one test sheet before printing many.

Printing on a PVC card printer

Choose PVC card printer on the Save & print screen. It makes a PDF with one card-size page for each side, which is what Evolis, Zebra, Fargo and similar printers expect. In the printer’s own settings, pick the card size (CR80) and turn off any scaling.

Sending cards to a print shop

With Pro, choose File for a print shop. It makes a 600 dpi PDF with 3 mm of bleed around the card and crop marks, which is what most print shops ask for.

Cards for a whole class or team

With Pro, open Batches and tap New batch, then pick the card design to use for everyone. Set the design up first, with your organisation’s name, logo and colours: every person’s card copies it.

  1. Tap Import spreadsheet and choose a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file. Columns such as Name, Roll no., Employee ID and Class are matched to the card’s fields for you, and you can change any match before importing.
  2. Tap Match photos and pick everyone’s photos at once. A photo is matched when its file name is the person’s ID, roll number or name, such as 1042.jpg, or when a spreadsheet column holds each photo’s file name.
  3. Tap Print all. IDcrest checks every card first, names anyone without a photo or with details that do not fit, and then prints sheets, a PDF for a card printer, or a ZIP of images named after each person.

You can also add people one at a time with Add person.

Making a photo look right

In Details, tap the frame button beside the photo, or in Design, select the photo and tap Frame. Drag to move it and pinch to zoom.

  • Find face centres the face and sizes it for an ID card.
  • Remove background cuts the person out and puts a plain backdrop behind them, such as white or passport blue. It works best with one person, in good light, against a background that stands out from them. Original brings the photo back as it was.

Both run on your phone, without an internet connection.

Saving your organisation’s details

Fill in the Organisation section of a card once: the name, logo, address and so on. Then tap Saved above it and choose Save these details for next time. On a new card, tap Saved and choose your organisation to fill them in. With one organisation saved, new cards start with it already filled in.

A barcode or QR code says the data does not fit

Each kind of barcode takes certain data:

  • EAN-13 needs 12 or 13 digits.
  • ITF needs an even number of digits.
  • Code 39 takes capital letters, digits and - . $ / + % and spaces.
  • Code 128 takes letters, numbers and most symbols, and suits most ID numbers.

For an ID with letters and dashes, such as EMP-2041, choose Code 128 or a QR code. A QR code holds more text, but a very long link may still not fit: shorten it. Before printing many cards, check that the code scans with the scanner you will use.

Writing in Urdu, Arabic or Hindi

Type names and details in any language. IDcrest includes fonts for Urdu, Arabic and Hindi, and lays the words out the right way round. If a name looks too small on the card, it has been shrunk to fit its box: make the box bigger in Design.

Where are my cards kept?

On your phone, inside the app. They are never sent to us. Like any app’s data, they are included in your phone’s own iCloud or Google backup if you have backups turned on. See the privacy policy for the few things that do leave the phone.

Moving to a new phone

Restore your phone’s backup on the new phone, and your cards come with the app. Google’s backup keeps at most 25 MB for each app, so on Android, if you have many cards with photos, also save PDFs of the ones you need. If you have Pro, open the Pro screen on the new phone and tap Restore.

I paid for Pro but it is not showing

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore, or tap Restore purchases in Settings, signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. A lifetime purchase never needs buying twice.

If it still does not appear, email us with the store you bought from and the date, and we will help you chase it.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us.

  • iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: Play Store, then your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.

You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If Pro ends, you keep every card and batch you made.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a small banner under the main screens and, now and then, a short ad after you save, share or print a card. Ads never appear while you are making a card. When a Pro action is for one card, such as one print-shop file, you can choose to watch a short video instead of buying Pro. Pro removes every ad.

On Android, in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you can change your choice about ads at any time in Settings, under Privacy choices for ads. On iPhone, IDcrest asks for non-personalised ads only and never asks to track you.

Usage counts and crash reports

They tell us which screens are used and why the app crashed, so we can fix problems. They never include a name, a photo, a card or anything you type. Turn them off in Settings, under Share usage counts and crash reports.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from, not by us. Apple’s are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com, and Google’s from your purchase history in the Play Store. Write to us if you get stuck.
