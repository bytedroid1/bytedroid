IDcrest

Professional ID cards for staff, students, visitors and events, designed and printed from your phone.

Coming soon to Android and iPhone

IDcrest makes professional ID cards on your phone, for an office, a school, a reception desk or an event. Pick a design, type the details, add a photo, and save, share or print the card at its true size. There is no account to create, and making cards works without an internet connection.

What it does

  • 35 designs for employee, student, visitor, event and membership cards, front and back, upright or sideways: office and site IDs, school and bus passes, parent pickup cards, hospital and reception visitor passes, press, exhibitor and delegate badges.
  • A form for the details, with sample text that shows where everything goes until you type your own: names, job titles, ID numbers, dates, blood groups, emergency contacts, and fields of your own.
  • Photos that look right: IDcrest finds the face and frames it, and can cut the person out of a busy background and put a plain, passport-style backdrop behind them. On the phone itself.
  • A full design editor: move, resize and turn anything, change fonts and colours, and add your logo, a signature, shapes, QR codes and barcodes (Code 128, Code 39, EAN-13, ITF, PDF417 and Data Matrix).
  • Names, details and labels in Urdu, Arabic and Hindi as well as English.
  • Save your school’s or company’s name, logo and address once, and fill them into every new card.
  • A check before printing: text too close to the edge, a photo too small to print sharply, a barcode that cannot hold its data, or sample details still on the card.
  • Save to Photos as a high-resolution image, share a PDF that prints at exactly 85.6 × 54 mm, print on A4 or Letter paper with cut guides, or make card-size pages for a PVC card printer.

Free, and what Pro adds

Making, saving, sharing and printing single cards is free, with every free design and the photo tools, and with no watermarks. The free version shows a small banner and, now and then, a short ad after you save, share or print a card; never while you are making one.

IDcrest Pro makes cards for a whole class or team at once: import a spreadsheet, match everyone’s photos by file name, and print every card after one check. It prints up to 10 cards on an A4 page, two-sided and lined up for cutting, makes 600 dpi files and print-shop PDFs with bleed and crop marks, opens the Pro designs, and removes the ads. It is a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Privacy at a glance

Account
None. No sign-up.
Your cards
Cards, photos, logos, signatures and the spreadsheets you import stay on your phone and are never sent to us. A card leaves the phone only when you save, share or print it yourself.
Photo tools
Face framing and background removal run on the phone. On Android, Google's ML Kit reports basic performance figures to Google; your photo is not sent.
Advertising
Google AdMob, in the free version only. A small banner under the main screens, now and then a full-screen ad after you save, share or print a card, and a short video only if you choose one. On iPhone, non-personalised ads only and no tracking. Your cards are never shared. Pro has no ads.
Crash reports and usage
Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics receive crash reports and anonymous usage counts, such as whether the Pro offer is opened or bought. Never your cards, photos or anything you type, and you can turn them off in Settings.
Purchases
Through the App Store or Google Play, checked by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier. We never see card details.
