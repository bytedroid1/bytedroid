At a glance

Account
None. No sign-up.
What you log
Food, drinks, water, weight and your goals stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Nothing you log goes to advertising, crash reports or usage counts.
Advertising
Google AdMob, in the free version only, as a small banner at the end of the Diary and Progress pages. The app asks for non-personalised ads only, after Google's consent form where the law requires one. Premium has no ads.
Crash reports and usage
Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, for crash reports and anonymous counts of which screens are opened. Never what you eat, drink or weigh. Off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland until you turn it on, and you can turn it off anywhere in Me.
Purchases
Through the App Store or Google Play, checked by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier. We never see card details.

The short version: what you log stays on your phone. We never receive it. There is no account, and no server of ours for it to go to. This page covers the parts that do leave the phone.

Stays on your phone

  • Every food and drink you log, with its amount, meal and time
  • Your weigh-ins, water and fasting times
  • Your answers when you set up the app: your goal, sex, age, height, weight, activity and pace, and the targets worked out from them
  • Foods you create, your favourites, and your settings

If your phone backs up to iCloud or to your Google account, this is part of that backup, like other apps’ data. The backup goes to Apple or Google under your own account, never to us. Delete all data in Me removes everything from the phone, and so does deleting the app.

Leaves your phone

  • Advertising in the free version, via Google AdMob: a small banner at the end of the Diary and Progress pages. The app asks Google for non-personalised ads only. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may receive your advertising ID, your IP address, your device model and system version, which ads you see or tap, and diagnostic details about the ads. Nothing you log is ever shared with Google for ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Me, under Ad privacy choices. Premium has no ads. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Purchases via RevenueCat. The App Store or Google Play takes the payment, and we never see card details. RevenueCat receives the store’s receipt and an anonymous ID for the app on your phone, so that Premium can be restored. It never receives what you log. RevenueCat’s privacy policy.
  • Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics, when the app crashes or hits an error: the kind of error and where in the app’s code it happened, your phone’s model and system version, the app’s version, and an ID for this installation. The error’s own message is left out, so a report never carries a food’s name or anything you typed. Never what you eat, drink or weigh.
  • Usage counts via Google Analytics for Firebase: for example that the app was opened, which of its main screens are opened (Today, Diary, Progress, Me and the Premium screen), and whether Premium is bought, with an ID for the app on your phone, your phone’s model and system version, and your country. Never what you eat, drink or weigh, and never your advertising ID. They are never used for advertising.

Crash reports and usage counts are off in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland unless you turn them on, and you can turn them on or off at any time in Me, under Share crash reports and usage. Turning them off also resets the ID that Analytics uses. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. How Google handles this information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We sell no information.

Permissions

LeanCal asks for no permissions on your phone.

Your choices

Delete any food from your log, or everything in Me with Delete all data. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Turn crash reports and usage counts on or off in Me. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Health information

LeanCal is a wellness app. What you log about your food, weight and goals is health information, and it stays on your phone: it is never sent to us, to advertisers, or in crash reports or usage counts. The app gives general guidance, not medical advice.

Children

LeanCal is for adults 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
