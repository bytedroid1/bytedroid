LeanCal

Count calories in seconds, see what's left for today, and reach your weight goal at a steady pace.

Coming soon to Android and iPhone

LeanCal is a calorie counter that keeps the basics free and your log on your phone. Answer a few questions for a daily target, then log what you eat in a few taps and always see what’s left for today. There is no account to create.

What it does

  • Works out a daily calorie target from your goal, age, height, weight, activity and pace, with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, and shows you how it got there. Carbs, protein and fat targets come with it.
  • Search more than 6,000 foods from USDA FoodData Central, even without an internet connection. Pick several foods and add them to a meal at once.
  • Quick add calories when you don’t know the food, create your own foods, and keep favourites a tap away.
  • See every food’s calories, where they come from, and its fibre, sugar and sodium.
  • Today shows what’s left in a ring, with carbs, protein and fat, your meals, water and weight. The Diary shows your day hour by hour.
  • Progress shows your weight trend from your fourth weigh-in, your goal, and the days you logged this week.
  • Calm mode hides the numbers and tells you how the day is going in words.
  • A fasting timer, if you fast.

LeanCal gives general guidance on calories and nutrition. It is not medical advice, and it is for adults.

Free, and what Premium adds

Everything above is free, with a small ad at the end of the Diary and Progress pages.

LeanCal Premium removes the ads and adds a weekly check-in that adjusts your target to your real weight trend, a weekly report of your last 7 days, and full nutrition: vitamins and minerals for every day and every food, with protein and fibre minimums. It is a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or a monthly one.

Privacy at a glance

Account
None. No sign-up.
What you log
Food, drinks, water, weight and your goals stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Nothing you log goes to advertising, crash reports or usage counts.
Advertising
Google AdMob, in the free version only, as a small banner at the end of the Diary and Progress pages. The app asks for non-personalised ads only, after Google's consent form where the law requires one. Premium has no ads.
Crash reports and usage
Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, for crash reports and anonymous counts of which screens are opened. Never what you eat, drink or weigh. Off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland until you turn it on, and you can turn it off anywhere in Me.
Purchases
Through the App Store or Google Play, checked by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier. We never see card details.
