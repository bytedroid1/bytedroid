Most things are answered below. If yours is not, write to us and a real person will read it. Please say which phone you are using and what you were doing when it went wrong; it saves a round trip.

How is my daily target worked out?

From the answers you gave when you set up the app. LeanCal estimates what you burn in a day with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and your activity level, then takes off (or adds) enough for the pace you chose. It never goes below 1,200 kcal a day for women, 1,500 for men, or 1,350 if you would rather not say. When you set up the app, or answer the plan questions again, tap How we worked it out on your plan to see the numbers. It is general guidance, not medical advice.

Changing my target or pace

Open Me, then tap Edit on your plan. Tap a target to change it, or tap Answer the plan questions again to change your goal, weight or pace and get a new target. With Premium, the weekly check-in can also suggest a new target from your real weight trend; you choose whether to use it.

Adding several foods at once

In Add food, tap the + next to each food you want. They turn orange with a tick, and stay picked while you search for more. The bar at the bottom adds up the calories; tap Add 2 to lunch (or however many you picked) to log them all.

A food I eat is not in the list

Tap Create food and enter the calories, and the carbs, protein and fat if you know them, from the label. It is saved under My foods for next time. If you only know the calories, use Quick add.

Favourites

Open a food and tap the heart. It then appears under Favourites in Add food. Tap the heart again to take it off.

Where is my log kept?

On your phone, inside the app. It is never sent to us, and there is no account. If your phone backs up to iCloud or your Google account, the app’s data is part of that backup, like other apps’ data. See the privacy policy for the few things that do leave the phone.

Moving to a new phone

If you restore your new phone from an iCloud or Google backup of the old one, LeanCal comes back with your log. Otherwise, the log stays on the old phone, because there is no account to sync it. If you have Premium, open the Premium screen on the new phone and tap Restore purchases.

Hiding the numbers

Turn on Calm mode in Me. Today then tells you how the day is going in words, such as “Plenty left today”, instead of showing calories.

I paid for Premium but it is not showing

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

If it still does not appear, email us with the store you bought from and the date, and we will help you chase it.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us.

  • iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: Play Store, then your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If Premium ends, everything you logged stays, and the ads come back.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from, not by us. Apple’s are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com, and Google’s from your purchase history in the Play Store. Write to us if you get stuck.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a small banner at the end of the Diary and Progress pages. There are no ads on Today, while you log food, or with Premium. The ads come from Google, and the app asks for non-personalised ads only.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you can change your choice about ads at any time in Me, under Ad privacy choices.

Crash reports and usage counts

They help us fix crashes and see which screens are used. They never include what you eat, drink or weigh. Turn them on or off in Me, under Share crash reports and usage. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland they are off until you turn them on.
