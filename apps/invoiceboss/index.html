InvoiceBoss

Invoices and quotations, made on your phone and sent in a minute.

Get it on Google PlayDownload on the App Store

InvoiceBoss turns a finished job into a professional invoice your client can pay, in about a minute, on your phone. There is no account to create and no internet connection needed.

What it does

  • Invoices, quotations and proforma invoices in the same editor. An accepted quotation or proforma becomes an invoice with one tap.
  • Receipts: when an invoice is paid in full, send your client a receipt in the same design.
  • Automatic numbering, tax, discounts, part payments and recurring invoices.
  • 53 invoice designs, with your logo and signature on every one.
  • Email the PDF, share it, print it or save it.
  • A client list and saved items, so repeat work takes one tap.
  • See what you are owed the moment you open the app, and export everything to CSV.
  • Payment reminders on the day an invoice is due, and again if it is three days late. Your client never gets a message from the app.
  • Bill each client in their own currency, and print the invoice in English, German or Indonesian. Names, items and notes in Urdu or Arabic print correctly on the PDF.
  • Back up to a file, or move everything to a new phone over your own Wi-Fi.

Free, and what Pro adds

The free version is not a trial: it makes 10 invoices and 10 sends a month in two designs, with quotations, proforma invoices, receipts, clients, part payments, reminders and backups included. InvoiceBoss Pro adds unlimited invoices and sends, all 53 designs with colour and font options, other document titles such as Quote or Bill, recurring invoices and reports. It is a yearly subscription with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Privacy at a glance

Account
None. No sign-up.
Your invoices
Invoices, clients and prices stay in a private database on your phone. They are never sent to us.
Advertising
None. On Android the advertising ID is used only to count installs that come from an advertising campaign.
Crash reports and usage
On Android, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics receive usage events, crash reports, device details, identifiers and an approximate location from your IP address. Nothing from your invoices. The iPhone app sends neither.
Purchases
Through the App Store or Google Play, validated by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier. We never see card details.
