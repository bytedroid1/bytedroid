PDF Send

PDFs from your phone to any computer, over Wi-Fi.

Get it on Google Play

PDF Send moves a PDF from your phone to a computer without a cable, a cloud upload or a sign-up. Pick a file and your phone becomes a small private web server on your own Wi-Fi. Open the address it shows, or scan the QR code, in any browser on the other device, and the file downloads.

How it works

  1. Tap Send PDFs and pick a file.
  2. Open the short address on your computer. Any browser works.
  3. Download.

Nothing is installed on the computer. With no Wi-Fi around, your phone’s hotspot does the same job.

PDF tools, on the device

  • Compress. Shrink large scans before sending them.
  • Merge. Combine several PDFs into one, in the order you tap them.
  • Split. Keep only the pages you need.
  • Protect. Lock a file with a password and AES-256 encryption.
  • Preview. Read a PDF full screen before you share it.

Every tool runs on your phone. Documents are not uploaded to a server.

Free, and what Pro adds

The free version sends single PDFs up to 100 MB. One payment, with no subscription, removes ads and adds sending several PDFs at once, receiving from a computer, the PDF tools, and control over how long a share stays open.

Privacy at a glance

Account
None. No sign-in, and the app never asks for your name, email address or contacts.
Your files
Travel directly between your own devices over your local network. They are never uploaded to us or to any cloud service.
Advertising
Google AdMob, in the free Android version, which uses your advertising ID, device details and approximate region. Pro removes ads.
Usage statistics
Firebase Analytics, on Android, receives an app-instance identifier, device details and usage events. Your PDFs are never included.
Purchases
Handled by Google Play, and recorded by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier.
